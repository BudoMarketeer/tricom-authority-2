Founder & Chief Master Instructor

Jared Wihongi

Jared Wihongi is the founder of TRICOM (Triangle Combatives) and Chief Master Instructor of the system. He started as a nightclub bouncer, then spent 25+ years as a street cop, a SWAT operator and a Police Academy defensive tactics instructor.

During the Global War on Terrorism he was contracted as a US Army Special Forces combatives instructor. He holds the rank of Grandmaster (Grand Tuhon) in Pekiti-Tirsia Kali, and TRICOM operates in a strategic alliance with the Pekiti Tirsia Tactical Association.

He has taught law enforcement, military and protection professionals in more than 40 countries. Every TRICOM course can be taken at Operator level or as a Train-the-Trainer program.
